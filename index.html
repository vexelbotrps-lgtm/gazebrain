EyeSync

A LOCAL WINDOWS ACCESSIBILITY APP

Use your computer
your way.

EyeSync uses your webcam to support head-driven cursor control and simple facial gestures. Camera processing stays on your computer; the website never accesses your camera.

Download full EyeSync setup bundle ↓

Extract the ZIP and double-click EyeSync.bat. It uses your newest installed Python 3.10+ to install the app's dependencies and open EyeSync. Python and an internet connection are needed for setup.

Runs locally on Windows

IN THE APP

Two choices. You decide.

01

Calibrate

Optional. Runs gaze calibration, then smile samples, then wink samples. Each step must succeed before the next one starts.

02

Start EyeSync

Starts the tracker and command panel without forcing calibration. Cursor movement stays off until gaze calibration is complete; optional smile/wink samples do not block startup or live controls.

For voice input, set your speech tool’s push-to-talk hotkey to Tab.
